Connect Spotify

Your music. Your rules.

Stop letting algorithms decide what you listen to.

Connect your Spotify account and take control of your music discovery. Explore different genres, venture into neighboring styles, dig into lesser-known tracks, or turn up the hits. You decide where the music goes.

Knarly picks the songs and the Spotify app on your phone or computer plays them. It needs Spotify Premium.

  1. In your app at developer.spotify.com/dashboard, open Settings, then Edit.
  2. Add this exact address as a Redirect URI and save:
  3. Your Client ID is already filled in below.

Where Spotify plays

Knarly sends songs to Spotify, which plays them on whichever speaker you choose. Sonos, Spotify Connect speakers and other devices show up here. Bluetooth speakers and car audio appear as the phone or car they’re connected to.

Library

Stations

Name each station with a genre. Type any name you like, or pick one from the list.

Where songs come from (your playlists)

Tag a playlist with a genre and every song in it joins that station. Spotify only lets apps read playlists you made or collaborate on.

Sort by artist

Give an artist a genre once and all their songs follow. Add a second genre for artists who fit two stations, like pop rock on both Pop and Rock.

Automatic star ratings

Spotify doesn’t share stream counts with apps, so Knarly rates songs from Last.fm’s public play counts instead. Stars you tap yourself always win.

Play gaps

About how often one song can come back, at the Balanced popularity setting. The Popularity dial stretches or shrinks these.

Neighbor genres

Choose which genres join a station, in order, as you turn Genre focus toward Adjacent: the first neighbor joins first, then the second, then the third. Leave a station on Built-in to keep Knarly’s own choices.

BPM (tempo) from Deezer

Knarly looks up each song’s tempo once in Deezer’s free catalog and saves it. Used by the Slow to fast and Party build orders. Some songs aren’t on Deezer or have no BPM there.

Add or fix BPMs opens this station’s songs. Type a BPM in any song’s box; your number always wins over Deezer’s. Clear the box to go back to Deezer’s.

All station playlists (optional)

Refreshes every station’s “Knarly” playlist at once (about four hours each), instead of one at a time with Send to Spotify.

Sonos helpers (optional)

Lets Knarly play on Sonos speakers that Spotify blocks, like a speaker on a Sonos Amp. Each house needs its own helper running there.

Copy setup link fills a helper in on another device with one tap. Keep links private: they contain the key.

Diagnostics (for troubleshooting)

Shows what Spotify reports, which devices Knarly can see, and where each command was sent.

Playback tests: start a song in the Spotify app first, then tap one test. Start the song again before the other test.
